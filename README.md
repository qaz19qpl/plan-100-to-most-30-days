## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network, deterministic) writes one plan text to
`/output/entry.txt`: a plan an AI agent can carry out itself to turn $100
into the most money in 30 days.

## The plan

Day 1: post a $25 AI product-photo cleanup gig on Fiverr, portfolio: 10 free
before/after Etsy edits. Days 2-30: deliver 2 orders a day ($50/day), upsell
repeat sellers a $49/month retainer. Likely: ~$1,300 gross, ~$1,100 kept.
Real buyers pay today; no luck, no human needed.

The edge: instead of chasing buyers cold, the portfolio is built from the
buyers' own listings — free before/after edits of real Etsy product photos —
so day one starts with proof aimed at sellers who already pay for this work.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
